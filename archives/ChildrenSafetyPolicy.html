Children's Safety Policy Statement

Our Commitment to Child Safety

We are committed to creating a safe, secure, and age-appropriate experience for children. Our app strictly adheres to Google Play’s Families Policy Requirements and applicable privacy regulations such as COPPA and GDPR-K.

Key Practices

  • No collection of personal information: We do not knowingly collect personal data such as names, email addresses, or location from children under 13.
  • Age-appropriate content: All in-app content is carefully curated to be suitable for children.
  • No inappropriate ads: We use only Google-certified ad networks and ensure that all ads shown are appropriate for children.
  • No unauthorized tracking: We do not allow third-party tracking or analytics tools for children’s profiles.
  • Clear privacy policy: Our full Privacy Policy is always available within the app and this website.

Parental Controls

We strongly encourage parents to be involved in their child’s digital experiences. Our app supports family-friendly settings, including:

  • Integration with Google Family Link
  • Restrictions on in-app purchases
  • Disabled or limited access to social features

Safe App Design

The application interface is designed with children's cognitive levels in mind. It avoids manipulative behavior, clickbait, or confusing UI patterns that may mislead children.

Data Compliance

We comply with all applicable laws and best practices regarding data collection and processing for children:

  • Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)
  • General Data Protection Regulation for Kids (GDPR-K)
  • Google Play Families Policy
